Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 29, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Maratha Business Hub ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application ("App").

By downloading, installing, or using the Maratha Business Hub app, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access or use the application.

2. Information We Collect

We collect several types of information to provide and improve our services to you:

  • Account Information: When you register an account, we collect your email address, password, full name, and phone number via Firebase Authentication.
  • Business Profile Information: If you register a business, we collect business names, category details, description, address/location, contact numbers (including WhatsApp), logos, images, and other promotional details.
  • Job Listing & Event Information: Information about job posts, employment requirements, application methods, community events, program schedules, and location coordinates.
  • Interaction Data: Visitor details tracking for listed businesses, allowing business owners to monitor page visits and connect with interested customers.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for various professional and operational purposes:

  • To facilitate easy connection and networking between Maratha business owners and prospective clients.
  • To authenticate your identity and provide secure login features.
  • To list, search, and map business locations for local discoverability.
  • To notify users of relevant updates, application features, new jobs, or community events.
  • To track profile visits so that listed businesses can optimize their listings.
  • To maintain and improve the overall performance, security, and user experience of our app.

4. Data Security and Storage

Your security is of paramount importance to us. We store your account and business data securely using Google Firebase Services (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which employs industry-standard encryption protocols. Photos and business logos are hosted securely through certified image-hosting servers (ImgBB) to enable fast loading speeds.

While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal information, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Third-Party Services

We may employ third-party companies and service providers to facilitate our App services, host user media, or analyze app metrics. These third parties have access to your Personal Information only to perform these tasks on our behalf and are obligated not to disclose or use it for any other purpose.

Our app utilizes the following third-party integrations:

  • Google Firebase: Used for secure authentication, real-time database management, dynamic configuration, and push notifications.
  • ImgBB: Used to securely host and retrieve user-uploaded images, such as business cards, logos, and event banners.
  • OpenStreetMap / Leaflet (Flutter Map): Used to provide geographical maps and business locating features without violating user privacy.

6. Location and Device Permissions

To provide location-based search results (finding shops or businesses near you), our app may request permission to access your device's geographical location using GPS. This location information is solely used to display businesses in your vicinity and is not tracked continuously in the background or shared with third parties.

You can enable or disable location access at any time through your mobile device system settings. Disabling location may, however, limit your ability to view nearby listings relative to your current position.

7. Your Rights & Data Deletion

We believe you should have full control over your personal data. At any time, you have the right to:

  • Access, review, or update your registered account or business listings directly inside the app settings.
  • Request the complete deletion of your account and all associated listings from our database.

To request permanent deletion of your data, you can contact our administration team at aniketatkare@gmail.com. We will process your deletion request within 7 business days, removing all authenticated credentials, listings, jobs, and events associated with your account.
